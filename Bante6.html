Shambolic, Jalandhar

Punjab · Late Afternoon

The school bell rings out across the fields. Bags thump onto the verandah floor, chappals kicked off at the door, and the smell of adrak chai rises from every courtyard in the village.

Under the great banyan at the centre of Shambolic, the ground is uneven and the dust is warm. This is the adda. Here, a boy's reputation is not built in the classroom — it is built with a flick of the thumb.

Glass kanche. A small hole in the earth called the gitta. A throw line six feet away. And four boys who will not go home until the light dies.

Traditional Rules

Dhamuli · Kanche
  1. The Pool. Every player contributes 3, 4 or 6 marbles to a common pool (max 30).
  2. The Throw. The player whose turn it is scoops the whole pool into the palm and throws it from the throw line toward the gitta. Some drop in; the rest scatter.
  3. The Challenge. The opponents pick the hardest marble — the one farthest from the gitta — and mark it. You must strike that marble first.
  4. The Flick. Drag backwards from your striker and release. Power and angle come from the length and direction of your pull.
  5. The Win. Hit the marked marble first and you take every marble on the ground plus everything in the gitta.
  6. The Miss. Miss it, or touch another marble first, and you only collect the marbles already resting in the gitta. The scattered ones stay for the next player.
  7. The Round. When the ground is empty, the pool is refilled and a new round begins. Marbles won are kept forever — the highest tally wins the Championship.

About & Deployment

Dhamuli Marbles v1.0

A single-file HTML5 / Three.js game. No backend, no build step, no external assets — every texture, every sound and every mesh is generated at runtime.

GitHub Pages deployment

  1. Create a public GitHub repository.
  2. Upload this file as index.html.
  3. Repository Settings → Pages.
  4. Source: Deploy from a branch → main → / (root).
  5. Save. Your game goes live at
    https://<user>.github.io/<repo>/

Controls — Desktop: click-drag with mouse. Mobile: touch-drag. Pull away from the striker to aim; release to flick. The guide line shows direction and power.

Built with Three.js · Web Audio API · Canvas 2D textures.
