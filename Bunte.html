Traditional Punjabi Kanche Rules

1. Gather the pot

Two to four players contribute 3, 4 or 6 marbles each. The total pot is capped at 30.

2. The opening throw

The first player gathers the pooled marbles in their palm and throws them towards the gitta (hole). Some fall into the hole while others scatter around it.

3. Select the challenge

The other players choose the hardest marble to hit. The game highlights that marble with a larger target ring and pointing arrow.

4. Thumb-and-index flick

The player uses the right hand, thumb and index finger to flick a striker marble towards the selected target. Drag backwards from the striker and release.

5. Winning

Successful hit: The player wins all remaining scattered marbles and any unclaimed hole marbles.

Miss: The player receives only the hole marbles. All scattered marbles remain on the ground for the next player.

6. Player rotation

Players take turns using the same remaining scattered marbles. Marbles are never awarded twice. The game ends when the scattered pot is won or exhausted.

Controls

Tap Throw from palm for the opening throw. During a flick turn, touch the striker marble, pull backwards and release. On desktop, use the mouse.
